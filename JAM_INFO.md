## Rating

48-hour rating period after submissions close. Criteria:

- **Enjoyment** — how fun it was to play
- **Presentation** — how well visuals, sound, colors, controls/gameplay work together
- **Art & Audio** — graphics and sound quality/style
- **Use of theme** — how well "Speed" is integrated
- **Use of prerequisite** — how well "Speed is your weapon!" is integrated

After the voting period, a judge panel assesses a bracket of games for the
category prizes above.

## Rules

- Keep content clean — no explicit, NSFW, or disturbing graphics.
- Keep game content in English (title may be in any language).
- Late entries/edits allowed for 6 hours post-deadline via Discord; **after that,
  game files cannot be edited at all**. To update an already-submitted entry
  within the late window, you must delete and resubmit through the late-submission
  channel, which may reset its ratings.
- You can't win 2 prizes in the same category (lower-ranked entry not considered).
- **Must be a member of the Micro Jam Discord to be eligible for any prize.**

## FAQ highlights

- **Teams:** only one member submits the game; add teammates to the game page.
- **Pre-made games:** not allowed — must be built in the jam window (not
  strictly enforced, but expected).
- **Premade assets:** allowed, with permission or if self-owned.
- **AI tools:** explicitly allowed and encouraged.
- **Following theme/prerequisite is optional** but both are rating criteria, so
  skipping either costs score.
- **Multiple awards:** yes — e.g. a top-3 overall finish *and* a Best
  Ziva/GameMaker Entry award can both be won by the same entry.

## How this informed BREAKNECK

- Targeted **pure web (HTML5)** specifically to qualify for Judge's Choice,
  which is scoped to the top 10 web games only.
- Did **not** use GameMaker, Ziva, or Easel, so the entry is intentionally
  **not** tagged `#gamemaker` / `#ziva` / `#easel` — those tags are reserved for
  the sponsor categories and tagging them without using the tool would be
  misleading.
- Built the core mechanic so that **theme and prerequisite are the same
  system**: your speed in km/h is literally your weapon (the damage number
  against block hardness) and literally the theme (steering authority falls off
  as speed rises, so speed is simultaneously power and danger).
- `ITCH_PAGE.md` explicitly narrates "How I used the theme" and "How I used the
  prerequisite" on the store page itself, since those are scored criteria most
  entries leave for the judge to infer.
- Plan to join the Micro Jam Discord and submit **early** (not at the deadline)
  since ranking depends on rating volume, and to rate ~20 other entries for
  reciprocity — full reasoning kept in the session's original plan file.
